Who we are

Crucible ("we") provides an AI tool that stress-tests decisions. Contact: replace-with-your-email.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • The decisions you submit and the verdicts we generate, so you can see your history.
  • Usage and billing metadata: run counts, token usage for fair-use limits, and (if you subscribe) a Stripe customer ID. Card details are handled by Stripe, not us.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address for rate-limiting and security.

How your decisions are processed

To generate a verdict, the text you submit is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for processing. We store your decision and its verdict in our database tied to your account. Do not submit information you are not permitted to share with a third-party AI provider. See Anthropic's terms for how they handle API data.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your plan and a customer ID; we never see your full card number.

Advertising (free tier)

The free tier may show ads served by a third-party ad network (e.g., Google AdSense). Ad providers may use cookies to serve and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent before personalized ads load, and you can choose non-personalized ads. Paid plans show no ads. If you are in the EEA/UK, personalized advertising requires a compliant consent mechanism (CMP), which the operator must configure before enabling ads in those regions.

Cookies

We use a strictly-necessary session cookie to keep you signed in and a token to protect against cross-site request forgery. Ad and consent cookies are only set if ads are enabled and you consent.

Your choices

You can request access to or deletion of your account and data by contacting us at the address above. You can change ad consent at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CPRA.
